Hygiene & Sterilization

Strict Clinical Standards & Patient Safety

We adhere to rigorous sterilization and safety regulations to ensure every patient receives treatment in a protected clinical environment.

Hospital-Grade Autoclaving

All reusable surgical-grade instruments undergo rigorous multi-stage autoclave sterilization monitored by biological indicators.

Single-Use Disposable Kits

Every PRP blood collection tube, sterile dressing, and suture kit is sealed and single-use, opened exclusively in front of the patient.

Continuous Staff Training

Our nursing and reception staff regularly undergo infection control refresher programs aligned with Sharjah Health Authority mandates.
